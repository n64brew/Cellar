N64brew Game Jam #5

Unlike previous years, the 2024 game jam only has one submission because it was a collaborative minigame jam. Everyone was given the same template and required to submit a minigame for the collection, similar to Pokemon Stadium's minigame Kids Club. The winner of this year's competition was Coin Rush. $1754 was raised for the Alzheimer's Association.
