N64brew Game Jam #5

Unlike previous years, the 2024 game jam only has one submission because it was a collaborative minigame jam. Everyone was given the same template and required to submit a minigame for the collection, similar to Pokemon Stadium's minigame Kids Club. The winner of this year's competition was Coin Rush, with SpaceWaves taking second and Hydra Harmonics taking third, all entries voted once again by the community. $1754 was raised for the Alzheimer's Association.

N64brew Summer Game Jam

For the fourth year in a row, N64brew hosted a game jam. This year the theme was *Summer, and the community voted on their favorite entries. Summer's Story got first place, the Megatextures demo got second, and Alien Sun got third. $776.76 was raised for the Michael J. Fox Foundation for Parkinson’s Research

N64brew Game Jam #2

The N64brew game jam returned for 2021, featuring Giles Goddard, Kaze Emanuar, LuigiBlood, and Samuel “Kaiser” Villarreal as guest judges. This year, the money was decided to be used for charity, with the winner choosing where the money would go. The theme was Control, and Wizard of the Board won this year, donating the prize pot of $1,254.38 to the Rainforest Trust. Mission Lost Control got second place while Voidblade got third.

N64brew Game Jam #1

In 2020, the N64brew Discord community hosted their first N64 homebrew game jam, with the theme Size. The jam featured judges David Doak, Allan Findlay, Neil Voss, Youtuber Snooplax, and N64brew user Buu342. The winner of the Jam this year was Telocation Gemini, taking home $676.26 of the $1,127.10 prize pool. Second place was tied by Kumi-Daiki Beatoff 64 and Sblobber 64, third place was tied by Lunar Assault 64 and The Swoop 64.
