Pyoro64 is a clone of a WarioWare minigame. It plays just like the original, but it has the addition of a two player mode.

Notably, the Pyoro64 ROMs were developed [alongside some documents](https://drive.google.com/drive/u/0/folders/1rOE2zYV2RPPx-2NHRGiGZ-RFx6w_6dAI) that cover how to use the official N64 SDK to make your first ROMs, using Pyoro64 as a practical example. The documents, however, remain unfinished.
